# Buy Box — Worked Example

> **Fictional.** Harbour Ridge Partners does not exist. This file is here so you
> can see what a properly filled box looks like before you fill your own.
>
> Note how specific the must-haves are, how the add-on platforms are named, and
> how section 4 admits what has already been ruled out. Those three things do
> most of the work.

## 6. Must-haves

1. Contract or recurring maintenance revenue above 40 percent of total, evidenced rather than described
2. A named second-tier manager who is not the owner and not the owner's child
3. In business 15 years or more under substantially the same ownership
4. Licensed in its state for the mechanical work it performs, licence current

## 7. Actionable ownership types

| Ownership type | In scope? |
|---|---|

| Family-owned, second generation or later | yes |
| Management-owned (MBO, no sponsor) | yes |
| Employee-owned / ESOP | yes, with caution on the valuation mechanic |
| Sponsor-backed (another PE firm owns it) | no |
| Corporate subsidiary or carve-out | yes, for a platform, if the parent is a real seller |
| Publicly listed | no |
| Ownership unclear | research it, do not approach until it is clear |

## 11. Notes

We have burned two quarters on banker processes in this sector and lost both on
price. Everything here exists to get in front of an owner before a banker does.

If a company looks right but the owner is 45 with two children in the business,
that is a WATCH forever, not a PRIORITY. Do not let a growth signal promote it.
